# Research (20 October 2026)

## Current site (vivaservice24.ch)
Services, prices and wording reused as is: fin de bail dès CHF 350 (vitres, stores et four compris), vitres 50–70/h, chantier 55–75/h, entretien 45–50/h, conciergerie 50–60/h, espaces verts 55–75/h, solaire dès 180 (eau pure par osmose, brosse douce, perche, nacelle), tags et spéciaux dès 250, urgence/week-end +20 à 50 %. Legal entity and IDE come from its mentions légales. Its legal page states the 5 videos are real interventions; all its photos are Unsplash stock (the hero "solaire" image is an illustration, not reused).

## Competitors reviewed
Romandie: dio-net.ch (Geneva, best example), vertnetgeneve.ch, fiditi.ch, idnettoyage.ch (lead platform), shine-fs.ch, batmaid.ch, plus carbonie.ch and maxiclean.ch in passing. International: Fantastic Services (UK), Simply Spotless (Sydney), TIDY (US), Bond Cleaning in Brisbane.
Lausanne price ranges seen: studio 250–430, 3 p. ~449–1'000, 5 p. 849–1'450. Viva's prices sit in the middle of the market.

## Patterns reused, and why
1. Headline about the état des lieux, not "cleaning" in general: matches the search and the deadline in the visitor's head (dio-net).
2. From-price visible in the hero ("Dès CHF 350, vitres, stores et four compris"): ad visitors want the price first.
3. Price table by pièces, the unit Swiss renters think in.
4. Instant estimate without contact details, then "Recevoir mon prix exact" pre-fills the form (vertnet).
5. Short 2-step form in the hero: service + pièces + date, then name + phone. E-mail optional.
6. "Envoyez des photos sur WhatsApp": easier on a phone than describing a flat (dio-net).
7. Fixed mobile bar Appeler · WhatsApp · Devis (dio-net), hidden while the form is on screen.
8. Google rating + count next to the CTA, and real reviews right under the hero (Simply Spotless, idnettoyage).
9. A detailed "Ce qui est inclus" checklist that doubles as "what the régie checks" (Fantastic, vertnet).
10. FAQ on the real objections: régie refuses, do I need to be there, how fast, weekend, payment.
11. Real local proof (team photos, real videos, a named local client) instead of generic stock.

## Avoided
- Unqualified "100 % garantie": no guarantee wording until the owner confirms the terms (see TODO-CONFIRM.md).
- Invented statistics, years in business, insurance claims.
- Placeholder text and off-topic testimonials; hourly-only pricing for fin de bail; long contact-only forms.
