# To confirm with the owner before (or soon after) the ads go live

1. **Legal details**: "Viva service24 Sàrl", IDE CHE-391.619.790, "non assujettie à la TVA" are copied from the current site's mentions légales. Confirm they are still correct.
2. **Guarantee if the régie refuses the cleaning**: the site only says "appelez-nous, nous trouvons une solution". If he offers a real guarantee (free reprise, within how many days, which items, conditions such as logement vide), we can add "garantie de remise" wording; it converts well when the terms are written out.
3. **Response time**: the site says "Réponse rapide". The old site said "sous 24 heures ouvrables". If he can commit to "Devis en 1 h" or "rappel dans l'heure", say so.
4. **Payment methods** (TWINT, cash, invoice, card?): the FAQ currently says they are in the quote.
5. **Real before/after photos**: ask for 5 to 10 real photos of end-of-lease jobs (kitchen, oven, bathroom, windows) to replace the two illustration pairs.
6. **Team / owner first names** (a review mentions "Kamiran"), and permission to show them; a short "who we are" with a face converts better.
7. **Exact zone he accepts**: the site lists the Lausanne towns + "tout le canton de Vaud", other cantons "sur demande".
8. **Fin de bail checklist**: the detailed "Ce qui est inclus" list (four, hotte et filtres, frigo, armoires, détartrage, stores, rails, radiateurs, balcon…) is the standard Swiss list. Confirm every item is included at the quoted price (hotte filters? cave/galetas? frigo dégivré by the tenant?).
9. **Studio price**: the estimator shows "dès CHF 350" for a studio (the general from-price). Confirm.
10. **Do I need to be present / logement vide**: FAQ answers are cautious ("pas forcément", "idéalement vide"). Confirm his policy.
11. **Insurance / "personnel déclaré et assuré"**: competitors use this; not on the site because unconfirmed.
12. **Night interventions**: one review mentions a booking for 01h00 and the listing says 24h/24; confirm he wants to advertise night work.
13. **Google Ads**: send the AW- ID and conversion labels, and decide if FormSubmit stays (activation e-mail must be clicked once, see README).
14. **Domain**: canonical URLs and the sitemap point to the GitHub Pages address. Switch BASE_URL in tools/build.py when the site moves to vivaservice24.ch.
